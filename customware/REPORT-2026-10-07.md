## Entry 014, a fork pushed to RAM stays shared: done

Plan `26d-bash-ocean-plaza` (`customware/014-shared-tails.md`). Engine (`88de1d50e`): a position window on the
attention cache's per-sequence state I/O, an attention-only state flag, `llama_memory_set_state_range`. Server
(`de2360b44`, `56b24a07b`): a conversation whose prefix another live sequence or a RAM segment covers is saved
as a tail (its private cells plus the recurrent state); the prefix is written once to RAM as a segment; on
restore the prefix re-attaches to a live holder (labels only) or comes back from the segment; a turn whose own
tail is in RAM is restored next to its idle sibling instead of taking it over. `LLAMA_TAIL_MIN` 4096.

112.6 MiB of it); the pair returns at 12833 cells instead of the 25230 two private copies need; neither side
